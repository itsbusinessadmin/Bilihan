Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

This policy explains what Bilihan ("we", "us") collects when you use this website to order food, why we collect it, how long we keep it, and what you can ask us to do with it. We handle personal information in line with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

1. Information we collect

Information you give us when you place an order

  • Your name — so we know whose order it is.
  • Your mobile number (optional) — so we can reach you about your order.
  • Your delivery address — only if you choose delivery. We do not ask for it for pickup orders.
  • Your preferred date and any note you add to the order.
  • Your payment receipt — only if you pay by QR and upload proof of payment.
  • Your cancellation reason — only if you cancel an order.

We do not collect card numbers, bank credentials, or any payment details. QR payments settle inside your own banking or e-wallet app, never on this website.

Information stored in your own browser

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. The store saves a small amount of data in your browser's local storage so it can work at all:

  • your shopping cart, so it survives a refresh;
  • your light/dark theme choice and product view preference;
  • your most recent order and its private cancellation token, so you can view or cancel it;
  • a cached copy of the menu, so you can still browse if your connection drops;
  • your answer to the cookie banner.

This data stays on your device. Clearing your browser data removes it, which also means you will lose the ability to cancel a recent order from this device.

Analytics

We only load analytics if you accept them in the cookie banner. If you do, we collect anonymous, aggregated page-view statistics — never your name, number, address, or order contents. If you choose "Necessary only", no analytics script is loaded at all. You can change your mind at any time using the cookie settings link.

2. How we use your information

  • To prepare, confirm, and hand over or deliver your order.
  • To contact you about your order if something changes.
  • To verify payment when you upload a QR payment receipt.
  • To keep our own sales and inventory records.
  • To handle cancellations and refunds.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for marketing unless you separately ask us to.

3. Who we share it with

Your order information is shared only with the service providers that run this store:

  • Supabase — hosts the database that stores products, orders, and order items.
  • Google (Apps Script, Sheets, Drive) — receives a copy of the order record and stores uploaded payment receipts for our bookkeeping.
  • GitHub Pages — serves this website.
  • Your chosen analytics provider — only if you accepted analytics.

We also disclose information where the law requires it, for example a lawful request from a government authority.

4. How long we keep it

Order records and payment receipts are kept for as long as we need them for our business and tax records. Cancelled orders keep the cancellation reason so we have a record of what happened. You can ask us to delete your personal details from our records at any time, subject to the record-keeping the law requires of us.

5. How we protect it

  • The whole site is served over HTTPS.
  • Database access uses Row Level Security: the public site can read the menu, but cannot read or edit other customers' orders.
  • Orders are created through a restricted database function, not by direct public writes.
  • Admin access requires a separate authenticated account that is explicitly listed as an admin.
  • Cancelling an order requires both the order number and a private token that only your browser holds.

No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable and appropriate measures to protect your information.

6. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to be informed, to access your data, to object to processing, to have inaccurate data corrected, to have your data erased or blocked, to damages, to data portability, and to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission. To exercise any of these, contact us using the details below and tell us your order number.

7. Children

This store is meant for customers who are able to enter into a contract. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us their information, contact us and we will remove it.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the "last updated" date at the top of this page. Continuing to use the store after a change means you accept the updated policy.
